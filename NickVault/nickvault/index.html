A LITTLE HISTORY

From three Nicktoons to a universe.

1990

Nickelodeon tests eight animated pilots while developing its original animation strategy.

1991

Doug, Rugrats and The Ren & Stimpy Show launch the original Nicktoons era.

1998

Nickelodeon opens its dedicated animation studio in Burbank and expands original animation.

1999

SpongeBob SquarePants premieres and becomes a global franchise.

2000s

The era of Fairly OddParents, Jimmy Neutron, Danny Phantom and Avatar arrives.

2010s–now

New generations arrive alongside reboots, spin-offs and streaming-era Nickelodeon productions.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A fan-made digital museum.

NickVault is a front-end project built with pure HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. It is designed as a portfolio-style site: responsive, interactive and easy to expand.

Important production note: television-series budgets are often not publicly disclosed. Where no reliable public figure is available, this archive says so instead of inventing a number. Movie budgets are separate and should not be presented as the cost of producing the television series.
